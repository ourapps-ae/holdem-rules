The pacing numbers are the recipe's constants, settable through the `pacing` argument.
- `implementation/server/holdem.ts`: chips move through chip-ledger operations (`commit`, `fold`, `endRound`, `returnUncalled`, `buildPots`, `awardPot`, `settle`, `voidHand`, `displayPots`, `nextHand`); the hand holds a `ledger` value and mirrors stack, wager and folded mark into its players after each ledger call. The recipe's `settle` 882-917 (uncalled excess, pots, awards) is now the ledger's `returnUncalled`, `buildPots`, `awardPot`, `settle`.
- `interfaces/game-rules.json`: the 14 operations of the turn-engine contract with typed arguments and returns, the lower layer's operations, settings and pacing. `interfaces/required/*.json`: the operations used of card-deck, chip-ledger, level-schedule, hand-ranking and rules-settings.
- Generalised: the recipe's `Game` mixed the hand with players, clocks and lifecycle; here a `Hand` holds only the hand (players with cards, stacks and wagers, deck remainder, board, street, stage, actor, bet to match). Every operation clones and returns a new `Hand`. The deck, blinds, level, previous positions, stacks and randomness arrive as arguments (the engine takes them from card-deck, level-schedule, chip-ledger and its own random source); the ranking is injected. The fixed 9 seats, the minimum opening bet of one big blind and revealing hands at the runout are settings (`seats`, `minimumOpeningBetBigBlinds`, `revealAtRunout`); the first two come from the house rules file (`rules/texas-holdem-house-rules.json`: players.maximum, betting.minimum_opening_bet_big_blinds), `revealAtRunout` is new (the recipe always reveals; default true). The runout is stepped by the engine (`runoutStep`) instead of by a clock inside the hand.
- Fix relative to the recipe: in `settle` the recipe builds pots with `buildPots` only. When every live player has put in less than folded players (for example two deeper stacks fold on the flop after a short stack is all-in), the folded chips above the top live layer match no pot and are never awarded. Reproduced against the recipe engine: three players, big blind with 100 chips, button raises to 300, small blind calls, big blind calls all-in, both others fold on the flop: 300 of 700 chips are awarded and 400 vanish. The recipe already knows the layout for display (`displayPots` in `view.ts` 131-141 puts such chips in the last pot); `settleHand` here awards from the ledger's `buildPots`, which puts such chips in the last pot (chip-ledger `buildPots` has the same fix), so no chip is lost. Covered by the test "chips of folded players above the last live layer are not lost" and by the conservation check in the random games.
- Left behind in the recipe (belongs to the batch that owns it): lobby, joining, names, kick, leave, sit out and return (`engine.ts` 209-310, 411-452), host and pause logic (`refreshPauses` 464-479, `isPaused`), clocks and the settle/decision/runout/result timers (`tick`, `dueStep`, `nextWake`, `setActor`, `SETTLE_MS` and the other constants at 9-38), absent-player handling, rebuys, eliminations, tournament completion and history (`finishHand` 925-941, `rebuy` 943-1017, `pushHistory`), hold/look/learn (`setHold`), prompts (`view.ts` 221-270), the odds rows in the view and the room adapter's lifecycle (`adapter.ts` `create`, `restore`, `join`, `presence`, `connection`, `advance`). `engine.ts` `act` still has no `show`-before-end or `muck` (the house rules forbid both).
- The pause per result kind (`SHOWDOWN_*_MS`) is now extracted: `outcome` returns it as `holdMs`.

## Tests and checks

- `tests/server.test.ts`, `tests/rules.test.ts`, `tests/web.test.ts`, `tests/support/*`, `examples/consumer.ts`: new. `tests/support/hand-ranking/*.ts`, `tests/support/chip-ledger/ledger.ts` and `tests/support/card-deck/deck.ts` are copies of the current poker-hand-ranking, chip-ledger and card-deck `implementation/server` files, used only to give the tests the real components. `tests/rules.test.ts` also carries an independent fake chip ledger written from the chip-ledger interface. When a provider changes, copy it again and rerun.
- `authoring/differential-vs-recipe.ts` (not packaged; run with the real chip ledger): a differential driver. It expects a copy of the recipe's `server/src/{cards,contract,evaluate,pots,rules,engine}.ts` and `rules/texas-holdem-house-rules.json` in a scratch folder (imports rewritten to `.ts`, the JSON import given `with { type: "json" }`), plays 150 seeded games through the recipe engine and this component with identical decks and the same random legal actions, and compares stacks, bets, street, actor, bet to match, last full raise, board, pots, winners, shown cards, last actions and positions after every action. Last run: 540 hands, 4409 actions (112 refused by both with the same code), 119 short all-in raises, 67 hands with side pots, 20 with split pots; the only difference was the chip loss described above.
